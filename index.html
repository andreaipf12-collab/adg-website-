How it works

Three steps.
One compliance package.

From GPS capture in the field to a DDS-ready PDF for your European submission — fully automated, satellite-verified.

1

Capture in the field

Field technicians collect all required data per farm using our offline-capable mobile app — location, labor indicators, and producer consent — all in one visit.

2

Verify by satellite

Our pipeline cross-checks every farm against satellite deforestation data. Post-2020 deforestation check. PASS / REVIEW / FAIL with a tamper-proof audit trail.

3

Deliver the package

A DDS-ready PDF with all three ESG pillars: Environmental (EUDR), Social (LkSG/CSRD), Governance. Ready to submit in EU TRACES NT.
