Why AgroDignity exists

The muscle
between two burdens.

Insight without income is advice.
Income without insight is charity.

The producer's burden
Proving origin costs labor the smallholder is never paid for — and the data it produces is worth more than they ever see.
The importer's burden
Proving origin under EUDR falls hardest on operators too small to carry a compliance department — with penalties of up to 4% of turnover.

AgroDignity sits between them. One verification, paid once, discharges both.

How it works

Three steps.
One compliance package.

From GPS capture to a DDS-ready file for EU TRACES NT — satellite-verified, starting with coffee, built for any regulated crop.

1

Capture in the field

One visit. A field technician records GPS boundaries, practices, and consent using an offline app — no paperwork, no repeat visits.

2

Verify by satellite

Every farm is checked against satellite deforestation data, post-2020. PASS, REVIEW, or FAIL — with a tamper-proof audit trail.

3

Deliver the package

A DDS-ready file covering EUDR, CSRD and LkSG — ready to submit in EU TRACES NT.
