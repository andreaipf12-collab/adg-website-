How it works

Three steps.
One compliance package.

From GPS capture in the field to a DDS-ready PDF for your European submission — fully automated, satellite-verified.

1

Capture in the field

Field technicians collect 16 CDAR data points per farm using our offline-capable mobile app. GPS polygon, labor indicators, FDRL consent — all in one visit.

GPS 6 decimals FDRL consent 16 CDAR fields
2

Verify by satellite

Our pipeline cross-checks every farm polygon against Global Forest Watch Hansen GFC data. Post-2020 deforestation check. PASS / REVIEW / FAIL with SHA-256 audit trail.

GFW Hansen GFC SHA-256 hash Immutable audit
3

Deliver the package

A DDS-ready PDF with all three ESG pillars: Environmental (EUDR), Social (LkSG/CSRD), Governance (FDRL). Ready to submit in EU TRACES NT.

DDS-ready TRACES NT 3 ESG pillars

Mission · Vision · Values

What we stand for.
Why it matters.

Mission
We bridge knowledge and communication between actors in the global food system — generating the capabilities for each actor to manage their own decisions and direct their own future through access to information and technology.
Vision
To be the pioneers in designing accessible systems where data from food systems generates value from its origin — returning that value to the people who created it.
01
Dignity
The capacity of every actor in the food system to manage their own decisions — with access to the knowledge, information and technology that make it possible.
02
Equity
Universal access to knowledge, information and technology — regardless of farm size or where in the world the producer is located.
03
Commitment
Building redistributive mechanisms within the data economy of food systems — ensuring that the value data generates returns to its origin.
04
Transparency
Every data point, every transaction, every decision is traceable and verifiable. No black boxes. What we say can be audited.
05
Trust
Built through clear communication and verifiable evidence — not promises. Every result is communicated openly to all actors involved.
